# Shape types in {{site.productNameTM}} Web Report Designer

The Shape report item in the {{site.productNameTM}} Web Report Designer supports the basic shapes listed below. You choose a shape type from the `Shapes` drop-down in the properties panel.

## Which shape types are supported?

The {{site.productNameTM}} Web Report Designer supports the following basic shapes.

  ![Shape Types](/static/assets/on-premise/images/report-designer/report-items/shape/shape-types.png '#width=450px')

| Types           |Description           |
|-----------------------|-----------------------|
| Ellipse               | A smooth, ellipse shape used to highlight content or add a decorative touch.|
| Triangle              | A three-sided shape often used to indicate direction, hierarchy, or to draw attention.|
| Right Angle Triangle  | A triangle with one 90° angle, typically used to suggest direction or represent corners.|
| Rectangle             | A defined area used for conditional formatting, interactive elements.|
| Hexagon               | A six-sided polygon symbolizing modularity, balance, or interconnected systems.|
| Pentagon              | A five-sided shape, often used for symbolic meaning or visual emphasis.|
| Octagon               | An eight-sided polygon commonly used to represent stops, warnings, or alerts.|
| Star                  | A multi-pointed decorative shape, often used for emphasis, highlights, or ratings.|
| Left Arrow            | Points left to show previous steps or backward navigation.|
| Right Arrow           | Points right to show next steps or forward movement.|
| Up Arrow              | Points upward to indicate increase, hierarchy, or upward navigation.|
| Down Arrow            | Points downward to show continuation, dropdown, or deeper detail.|
| Plus                  | Represents addition or aggregation operations between multiple data values.|
| Minus                 | Represents subtraction, reduction, variance, or exclusion operations.|
| Multiplication        | Indicates multiplication or scaling operations.|
| Division              | Represents division or ratio calculations.|
| Trapezoid             | A four-sided shape with one pair of parallel sides, often used for process flows or hierarchies.|
| Parallelogram         | A four-sided shape with opposite sides parallel, commonly used for data or process representation.|
| Equation              | Represents equality or assignment of calculated results.|
